The three moves that work on anything

Don't see your subscription above? These work regardless of who you're cancelling.

First

Find who actually bills you

Signed up in an iPhone or Android app? Cancelling inside that app often does nothing — the App Store or Google Play is the real billing authority. Check Settings → Subscriptions (iPhone) or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions (Android) before you do anything else.

Second

Say one sentence, and repeat it

If you're forced onto a call, open with: "I'm calling to cancel, effective today — please confirm in writing." Say it again, word for word, after every retention offer. You don't owe them a reason.

Third

Get it in writing, same day

A phone confirmation isn't proof. Ask for a cancellation or reference number, then follow up by email the same day summarizing the call. If you're in California — or another state with a strict auto-renewal law — say so; they're often legally required to make cancelling as easy as signing up.
